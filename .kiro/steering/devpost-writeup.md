# Devpost write-up rules

## Who reads it

Kiroween judges. They score Potential Value, Implementation (how well Kiro was used) and Quality and Design. Most read English as a first language and do not know Kazakh folklore or the Ladder method.

## Must have

- What it is in one sentence, then the problem (fears feel bigger when they are vague) and how the app helps.
- How Kiro was used, with real file paths: specs (`.kiro/specs/`), steering (`.kiro/steering/`), hooks (`.kiro/hooks/` and the agent hooks), MCP (`.kiro/settings/mcp.json`), custom agents, skills, the power and the release workflow. Say where vibe coding was used and where specs worked better.
- Pre-existing work: the Ladder MCP server at `https://108psy.com/mcp` was built before the hackathon. Everything in this repository is new.
- Links: the live demo, the public repo, the video (under 3 minutes).
- Kazakh words with the meaning first: "a copper-clawed witch (Zheztyrnaq)".

## Never

- Never call Aizhan Li a psychologist or a therapist. She is "author of the Ladder method".
- No prices, no subscription offers, no "per day" sums.
- No promise of a health or life result. Self-reflection tool, not therapy.
- No em dash, no arrow symbol, no middle dot. Use a full stop, a comma or a colon.
- Nothing is submitted until Aizhan Li has read the full text and said yes.
